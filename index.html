Research focus

Interplay between geometry and analysis

01

Free boundary problems

Monotonicity formulas, variational problems, and the geometry of one-phase and two-phase interfaces.

02

Geometric analysis

Green functions, curvature, conformal methods, and analytic inequalities on curved spaces.

03

Convexity & combinatorics

Section-projection comparisons, Brunn-Minkowski questions, and dimension-free sumset inequalities.
